# PleaseBeReady promotion plan (draft, Oct 28, 2026)

Materials only. **Nothing here has been posted, sent or paid for.** Rules and sources are in `STOCKUP-RESEARCH.md`.

## Ground rules

- **Disclosure:** every post that carries an Amazon link starts with "Paid link:" or ends its first line with "I earn a commission if you buy through links in this post." Never only in hashtags (FTC). Posts that only link to pleasebeready.com need no affiliate disclosure, but the site shows it beside every shop button.
- **Link to our site, not Amazon:** posts point to `pleasebeready.com/planner?start=<situation>`, which opens a plan of that kind. Amazon links live on the site.
- **No paid search pointing at Amazon:** any paid test sends traffic to pleasebeready.com only and never bids on "amazon" (Associates Participation Requirements §6(f)). The tests below are organic; paid tests wait for an owner decision on budget.
- **Copy rules:** no fear, no countdowns or "before it's too late", no promises of safety, no invented numbers. Plain words; the planner's math is the hook.
- **Confirm before posting:** each social profile is listed as a Site in the Associates account (manual check).

## Channels

1. **Instagram (existing account and publisher).** The site already posts the Daily Readiness card through the Graph API. Add planner posts as carousels: a checklist slide, then "your amounts" for an example group. Links go in bio and story link stickers.
2. **Pinterest (new, organic).** People save checklists and packing lists, and pins keep circulating. Each pin is a tall checklist image linking to a `?start=` page. *Reasoning, not measured: confirm with Pinterest's own rules and analytics.*
3. **Facebook (local groups, as a person, never spam).** Share only where groups allow it, such as parent groups before school or neighborhood groups before a storm. Link to the site only.
4. **Short video (Reels/TikTok), later.** "Plan for 12 kids at a sleepover in 20 seconds" screen recordings. Start only once 1–3 show what lands.

Under 10: the landing page needs a clearer first step. |
| 2 | Homeowners before a forecast storm | "Storm list for your household in 2 minutes" | ?start=winter or ?start=hurricane | Clicks and plans in the 48 hours before the event versus a normal week | A big lift means timing beats theme: keep seasonal posts on a weather calendar. |
| 3 | Pinterest searchers | Tall checklist pin vs "planner screenshot" pin, same situation | ?start=camping | Outbound clicks per 1,000 impressions | Use the winning pin format for the next 10 pins. |
| 4 | Adult children of older parents | "Checking on a parent this winter" | ?start=caregiving | Plans started; share-link use | Strong sharing means it's a family conversation: add a "send to a sibling" angle. |
| 5 | Everyone | Search button vs a named product in the caption | Same page | Shop clicks per plan; Associates conversion by tracking ID | If named products convert far better, verify more ASINs by title for top items before naming them. |

**Measure with what exists:** the site's GA events `gear_click` and `need_select`, plus Associates reports. Use separate Associates tracking IDs per channel only after confirming in the account how to create them.
