# PleaseBeReady (pleasebeready.com)
Be ready for whatever is coming: shopping kits and saved Ready Lists for any situation (outages, storms, pets, cars, trips, parties, moving, caregiving) plus calm emergency checklists. No doomsday. Secular. Brand (Oct 31, 2026): orange **#F97316** on navy, bag-and-check mark + Archivo wordmark in `components/Brand.tsx` (same shapes in `app/icon.svg`, `public/icons/`, `OgCard.tsx`, `docs/social/brand/make.py`).
- Keep the "not professional or medical advice" disclaimer above the footer.

## Open Mirror family rules
- One of 11 Open Mirror LLC sites (hub: openmirrorllc.com, repo djvaughn22/open-mirror). Baseline tag: `mvp-1`.
- **Design:** flat + cool. bg `#0b1220`, surface `#141d2e`, border `#26324c`, text `#e8edf5`, muted `#94a3b8`. No glass, no gradients, **no red**.
- **Shared chrome is SYNCED, not owned here:** `OpenMirrorNav.tsx`, `OpenMirrorFooter.tsx`, `OpenMirrorTheme.tsx` in the app folder are copies from the hub repo `packages/openmirror-ui/`. NEVER edit them here — edit in the hub, run its `scripts/sync-ui.sh`, then rebuild/commit each satellite.
- Nav + footer mount in `layout.tsx`. Footer = OPEN MIRROR LLC · ABOUT · ✝️ ❤️ 🙏 (the icons ARE the CrossHeartPray link — no word).
- ☀️/🌙 family toggle (`om-theme`) lives in the bar; pages that compute JS colors follow the `om-theme` window event.
- **Copy style:** DJ's words. Short, plain, human. Never wordy or AI-sounding.
- **Deploys:** push to `main` = production deploy (Vercel). Batch related edits into one commit.
- **Two kinds of help, never blurred (owner direction 2026-10-07, updated 2026-10-17):** checklists and tools (checklists, guides, supply calculator) and gear picks (Amazon affiliate, folded under each section, disclosure beside the links). No "free", "paid tools" or price wording in public copy (2026-10-17) — describe what visitors can do; keep every Amazon link, tag and commission notice. Never add a price, checkout, or "coming soon" product without a real, working offer. Checklist content lives in `app/lib/readiness.ts`; every advice line must match its linked official guide. Free tools stay on-device (test-locked in `app/lib/readiness.test.ts`). Re-check ASINs by title, not status code.
- **Shop (owner direction 2026-10-30, "Amazon-first shopping destination"):** home `/` = storefront, no headline or pitch (owner direction 2026-10-31): wordmark + search, 16 department tiles (Emergency first), Ready Deals (only verified discounts from `lib/shop/deals.ts`, empty until the Creators API; section hidden when empty), Ready Finds tabs (Essentials default = `CORE` in `lib/shop/priority.ts`; New = picks `added` in the last 60 days, newest first; Everyday weekly rotation; Budget picks; Upgrades — from `lib/shop/finds.ts`), kits rail, Continue shopping, this month's kits and guides; no product twice. Department pages (`/shop/[lane]`, `LaneShop.tsx`): ranked essentials (`laneFeatured` in `lanes.ts`; party goods never lead), kits and guides, budget/our pick/upgrade, then everything with sidebar filters. Product art = drawings in `components/shop/Art.tsx` (no Amazon photos until the Creators API). "Our pick" shows only when an item has other choices (`earnedBadge`). Saving: inline `ListPicker` + a 3.5 s `SaveToast` (`SaveBar.tsx`), never a fixed bar over products; old home checklist lives at `/checklist`. Data in `app/lib/shop/`: `kits.ts` (31 hand-picked kits over catalog ids: must-haves, upgrades, budget swaps, easy-to-forget, to-dos, search terms, months), `lanes.ts` (16 lanes as rules over category/situation/needs, no duplicate records), `picks.ts` (the ONLY registry of exact products: 204 ASINs, each with role, our name, reason, use, evidence `via`/`seen` and `checked` date; add rows with `npm run picks -- add` after a fresh check, never without evidence; `npm run picks -- due` lists re-checks; workflow in `docs/CATALOG.md`), `search.ts` (phrase search, no AI: numbers → group/length; a typed word that names the item itself, like "water" → Bottled water, outranks one that only describes it; `shop.test.ts` locks the 12 example phrases), `kitPlan.ts` (kit → plan math). Pages: `/kits`, `/kits/[slug]`, `/shop`, `/shop/[lane]`, `/search` (noindex). Save buttons go through `components/shop/Saver.tsx` + `ListsProvider` into the same store as the lists.
- **My Ready Lists (`/lists`; was the stock-up planner at `/planner`, which 308-redirects; owner direction 2026-10-28/30):** many saved lists per device (`pbr:stockup:v1` key kept, stored object now `{ v: 2, plans, activeId, targetId }`; v1 data loads unchanged). Per item: amount, have, Bought, Ready (put away), Restock (used up), note; per list: note, kit. "Bought" is only ever the visitor's own tick, never inferred from a click. Share links carry the list in `#plan=`; `#list=<id>` opens a saved list. Catalog = hand-written base in `app/lib/stockup/catalog.ts` plus line-format files in `app/lib/stockup/data/` (format in `data/parse.ts`; 1,052 entries; `catalog.test.ts` enforces ≥1,000, no duplicate or near-duplicate names, valid tagged links, sane rules). Demand research and Associates rules: `docs/STOCKUP-RESEARCH.md`; social plan (materials only, nothing posted): `docs/PROMOTION-PLAN.md`. `/lists?start=<situation>` opens a new list of that kind (other query params such as utm_ are kept), math in `calc.ts` (every amount shows its arithmetic and says "our planning rule" unless it carries a `source`). Shop links (`stockup/shop.ts`): an item's first pick → its product page, button "Shop on Amazon" (solid); otherwise a tagged Amazon *search* (`amazonSearchUrl` in `gear.ts`), button "Search Amazon" (outlined) with its search words shown. Catalog items carry no `asin` (`data/parse.ts` rejects `asin=`); checklist gear ASINs must exist in `picks.ts` (tested). One card everywhere: `components/shop/ItemCard.tsx`. Accent borders use `.pbr-edge` (globals.css), never an inline border-color: the light theme turns any inline "color: #F97316" (border-color included) into dark orange text. Every page prints black on white (globals.css). No multi-item cart, prices, ratings or stock claims. "Good to know" commission notice sits beside every shopping area. GA events (labels only, never typed words): plan_start, plan_add, shop_click, save_item, save_kit, search, list_share.
- **Get Ready (`/ready`, owner direction 2026-10-31, "guided readiness system"):** a guided plan is a Ready List with `guide` set (`{ pace: now|weekly|monthly|custom, every, size, started }`); lines gain `bought` for partial buys (`toBuy` subtracts it). Engine `app/lib/ready/engine.ts` (tests in `engine.test.ts`, the 11 brief scenarios): emergency situations use the Ready.gov kit order in `KIT` (water → food → light/power → health → alerts → sanitation → shelter/leaving → extras), a default shopping order that hazard rules override (`hazards()`: medical power, evacuation, cold, heat move items to "critical" = always step 1); every other situation uses its own essentials → useful → extras, never emergency priorities. Tiers: critical/essential count toward "% ready" (by amount covered, not ticks); recommended is scheduled after; upgrades are never counted or scheduled. Slow paces build a 3-day base of every essential first, then raise daily amounts through 1 wk / 2 wk / 1 mo… to the plan length; step 1 always holds critical items plus the first water and food; `WITH` pairs (can opener with food, batteries with flashlights) ride together. Budget = things per trip (no prices on the site). `COUNTS_TOWARD`: filled containers count toward water, food kits toward person-days. Setup never inflates what a visitor owns when people/days change. UI: `components/ready/` (`ReadySetup`, `ReadyPlan`, home `ReadyCard`, `PlanGaps` on kit and department pages = "Add to my plan" for only the gaps). Meds/diabetes/pet meds get no store link.
- **One preparedness ranking (owner direction 2026-10-31, "necessities first"):** `app/lib/shop/priority.ts` (`prepTier`/`prepRank`/`byPrep`/`spread`; 12 tiers: water, food, lighting, power, first aid, radio, sanitation, fire safety, shelter/warmth, tools, convenience, upgrades; tests in `priority.test.ts`). It drives home Essentials, the Emergency department (`prep: true` lane), emergency kits' must-haves/upgrades (`kits.ts` re-sorts kits whose base is an emergency situation), Get Ready's order within stages, and broad readiness searches (every word in `BROAD` or the matched kit's own words; specific searches like "shower caddy" keep relevance scoring). Accessories that live in necessity categories go in `CONVENIENCE`. Non-emergency departments keep their own order. Every pick has an `added` date (`npm run picks -- add` writes today); never relabel old products as new.
- **Water and food are supplies, not equipment (owner direction 2026-10-31):** `app/lib/ready/supplies.ts` is the one definition. Water = gallons on the `water` line (have = stored, bought) + bottled water by the bottle + containers ONLY by `line.filled` (an empty container is never water; tablets and filters never count). Food = person-days on `shelf-food` + a grocery mix (`FOOD_MIX`: canned meals, proteins, beans, vegetables, fruit, shelf-stable milk, peanut butter, crackers, bars), each kind capped at its share so 40 cans of beans never make a week; ration packs count by label calories (Datrex 3600 = 1.8 person-days at 2,000/day). These ways have tier `way` in the engine: counted through water/food, never scheduled or counted on their own. The Get Ready Water and Food cards (`components/ready/Supplies.tsx`), home card, Lists header and kit item cards all read these numbers. Never claim complete nutrition, a price, or that a filter makes every source safe.
